## Why this exists

This post is a small integration test for the writing system. It should support
math, citations, and code without turning the site into a heavy application.

Inline math works: $L(\theta)=\mathbb{E}_{x \sim D}[\ell(f_\theta(x), y)]$.

Block math works too:

$$
\nabla_\theta L(\theta) =
\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\nabla_\theta \ell(f_\theta(x_i), y_i)
$$
